  • Title — animated belt, PRESS ENTER OR CLICK TO START
  • Menu — START SHIFT · HOW TO PLAY · SETTINGS

  • Pause — P / ESC (resume, restart, quit)
  • Game over — rank S/A/B/C/D/F, stats, NEW BEST crown

📦 How to play

  1. TAPE IT — SPACE or click a parcel. Fragile needs 2 tapes.
  2. SORT IT — in the SORT ZONE press A S D / 1 2 3 for RED / BLUE / YELLOW.
  3. OR DRAG IT — grab any parcel and drop it on the right truck.
  4. DON'T BLOW IT — wrong truck or parcel falling off = 1 strike. 3 strikes = shift failed.

Golden parcels = +500 · taped + correct = combo bonus · un-taped = only +30
